Command a rabble of Norse gods through a series of giant puzzles to retrieve the [Gjallarhorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gjallarhorn) and bring back balance to the [Bifröst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bifr%C3%B6st).

Solve puzzles by dragging the gods from your hand and onto the board to attack or perform powers. If a god or giant's health reaches zero, then they'll move into the grave. The level ends when there are no giants left on the board.

<img alt="A preview of the game that shows Heimdall fighting giants" height="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/danprince/gjallarhorn/refs/heads/main/preview.gif" />

## Tips
- Don't be afraid to reset and try a completely approach!
- Pay close attention to how much health the giants have, so you know where to focus.
- If you get completely stuck, you can use the level select buttons to skip a level and continue with the story.

## Editor
If you enjoyed the game, you can create more puzzles with the [level editor](https://gjallarhorn.danprince.me/?edit).

- <kbd>1-9</kbd> to set the card under the cursor.
- <kbd>shift</kbd>+<kbd>1-9</kbd> to set the health of a card.
- <kbd>esc</kbd> or <kbd>x</kbd> to erase the card under the cursor.

Whilst editing, the state of the level is saved in the URL so that you can share your puzzles.
